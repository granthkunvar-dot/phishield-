Two-Layer Explainable Hybrid Architecture

Combining deterministic rule-based heuristics with machine learning to eliminate single-model failure modes and automation bias (Parasuraman & Riley, 1997).

Layer 1: 13 Structural Heuristics (40% Weight)

Inspects verified syntactic threat indicators including IP address hostnames, deceptive @ delimiters, brand name impersonation, suspicious TLDs, and homoglyph attacks. Every triggered indicator generates an explicit plain-English explanation.

Grounded in Zhang et al. (2007) & Mohammad et al. (2014)
Layer 2: Random Forest Classifier (60% Weight)

Extracts a 26-dimensional tabular numerical feature vector (length, entropy, delimiter frequencies) evaluated through a 100-estimator Random Forest ensemble trained on the PhiUSIIL benchmark (Prasad & Chandra, 2024a).

Validated against empirical Logistic Regression baseline
Ethical & Legal Principles (PPD Section 8.1): Phishield outputs are strictly advisory recommendations to assist non-technical users at the moment of decision. In compliance with UK GDPR Articles 5(1)(c) and 5(1)(e), sensitive URL parameters are automatically masked (***MASKED***) and stored histories are automatically purged after 90 days.
