Research Methodology

About Phishield

Combining structural heuristic rules and Random Forest machine learning to make phishing detection transparent, secure, and explainable to non-technical users.

Research Aim & Question

Aim: To design, implement, and evaluate a website that detects phishing URLs by combining rule-based heuristics and machine learning techniques, explaining its results clearly to non-technical users.

Research Question: Can URL rules combined with a Random Forest on URL-only features reach at least 0.90 phishing precision and recall on unseen URLs, with reasons non-experts can follow?

The 13 Transparent URL Heuristic Rules

Grounded in foundational studies (Zhang et al., 2007; Mohammad, Thabtah & McCluskey, 2014) and the PhiUSIIL benchmark (Prasad & Chandra, 2024a), Phishield inspects 13 distinct structural indicators:

  1. IP Address in Hostname (R1): Flags raw numerical or hex/octal IP addresses used instead of registered domain names.
  2. URL Length Analysis (R2): Identifies abnormally bloated URLs (> 75 chars) used to mask parameters or evade small mobile screens.
  3. URL Shortening Service (R3): Detects known link redirectors (e.g., bit.ly, tinyurl) used to hide the true host.
  4. Deceptive "@" Symbol (R4): Flags authentication delimiters that trick browsers into ignoring preceding authority text.
  5. Double Slash Redirection (R5): Detects consecutive slashes ('//') in path/query sections used in open redirects.
  6. Hyphens in Domain (R6): Identifies hyphenated patterns commonly seen in lookalike brand domains.
  7. Subdomain Nesting Depth (R7): Evaluates excessive subdomain stacking (3+ subdomains) mimicking legitimate organizations.
  8. Uncommon Web Port (R8): Flags non-standard ports (outside 80 and 443) that bypass standard reputation controls.
  9. Security Token in Hostname (R9): Catches misleading keywords ('https', 'http') baked into hostnames to falsely imply security.
  10. Brand Name Impersonation (R10): Matches high-target financial and service brand names on unauthorized hosts.
  11. High-Abuse TLD (R11): Flags top-level domains statistically over-represented in disposable spam and fast-flux infrastructure.
  12. Excessive Special Characters (R12): Detects high frequencies of delimiters and percent-encoded characters used for obfuscation.
  13. Punycode / Homograph Attack (R13): Identifies Internationalized Domain Name prefixes ('xn--') and visual homoglyphs.

Machine Learning & Tabular Feature Engineering

Phishield extracts 26 purely structural, URL-only numerical features (lengths, character distributions, ratios, and Shannon entropy values).

Baseline Classifier

Logistic Regression trained on tabular features to establish an empirical benchmark.

Production Classifier

Random Forest Ensemble (100 estimators, max depth 15) achieving > 0.90 precision and recall with sub-millisecond inference latency.

Hybrid Decision Weighting: To achieve transparent explainability without susceptibility to single-model evasion, the final assessment combines the 13 heuristic rules (40%) and the Random Forest probability (60%).
